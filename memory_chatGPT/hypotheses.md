# My working hypotheses and saved cases

Memory topic: §chatgpttheories. Initial notes: 11 October 2026.

These are my provisional models for finding candidates in this project's texts. They are neither established translations nor independently verified explanations of glossolalia. The author's religious account, my reconstruction and evidence for a language component remain distinguishable.

## 1. Compressed, layered relationships

My useful working model is that some records combine an address, an action or state, a target such as «едро», and a result marker. Looking for relationships between those components can be more productive than trying to expand every syllable into an ordinary sentence.

This extends the author's idea of compressed roots and biblical references. It is still only a search model. It should account for repeated positions and contrasts in new records; if I must add most of the relationships from the prayer's theme, that would weaken the model.

## 2. A retained consonant scaffold with changing vowels

The author observes that a vowel change can point to the same base with a changed ending or form, giving «Илохим» versus «Елохим» as an example. My practical hypothesis is to search for a retained consonant scaffold first and compare vowels, pauses and endings afterwards.

I must not turn that into permission to interchange any vowel or to assign a grammatical case without evidence. A candidate becomes stronger when the same alternation correlates with an independently identifiable context across several records. One persuasive resemblance is insufficient.

## 3. More than one scope can remain useful

Two readings can be logically compatible: the desired order is preserved, while the disorder or its support is lost. For T00061 the author explicitly suggested retaining both possible scopes of negation. My interpretation is that this is a useful layered reconstruction; I cannot establish from that agreement that the transcription deliberately encoded both readings.

Keep the branching analysis visible before writing the coherent version. The risk is that a satisfying pair of meanings can hide an unexplained morphology.

## 4. Technical vocabulary can function as a metaphor

English-looking fragments such as `set`, `lost`, `check`, `reset` and `render` make a model of checking, fixing or restructuring an inner state worth exploring. This fits several contextual readings in the chat. It does not establish a real program, an instruction channel for AI or an external causal mechanism.

In particular, `nope` and `no-op` are different candidates. `rest` and the author's possible `reset` are different candidates. `info-exist-check` can suggest a check for information; it does not report the result of that check. Recognisable technical vocabulary must not lend its apparent precision to the unresolved remainder of a word.

## 5. Current particle meanings must not be overwritten by an old theory

Author clarifications retained in the local [short-word owner](../docs/dictionary/abbreviations-source.txt) at this snapshot include «Фа! / -фа» as «совершённый факт, уже факт» and «фи» as «на подходе, грядёт, в состоянии приближения». The v4 analysis also proposes «Фе» as «на пороге / свершается». Its possible sequence фи → фе → фа is a context-specific hypothesis, not an established universal tense system.

Earlier «Фа = здесь» and «фи = fear» readings remain in historical material. Consult the dated dictionary contexts and the author's later clarification rather than giving an old expanded theory priority over every subsequent correction. If the checkout's dictionary has not yet incorporated this chat's clarification, flag that mismatch rather than silently erasing either history.

The same principle applies to «фиктим»: fixing or shaping, fact-like readings and a sacrificial reading have appeared in different analyses. The latest context does not by itself cancel all earlier possibilities.

## 6. Reader-supplied meaning is a rival explanation

A clear spiritual interpretation may be supplied by the known prayer topic, the listener's expectations or a broad resemblance. That is a competing explanation I should try to rule out before calling a result a translation.

My proposed comparison is to parse without the intended topic, state candidate boundaries and meanings in advance, and see whether the same rules work on another record. Compare alternative languages and segmentations too. This notebook records the proposed method; no successful blind test is claimed here.

| Candidate | Why I kept it | What is not explained |

| «не / ордо / ви / тениум / lost» | Latin ordo, vī and teneō / teneam suggest order, force and holding; English lost suggests loss | The whole form, boundaries, syntax and «-тениум» are unestablished |
| «не / ордо / вите / ниум / lost» | Ordo + vita suggests an order of life | «-ниум», the vowel pattern and exact negative scope are unresolved |
| A vitium association | A fault, defect or vice would fit a damaged-order theme | The recorded consonants and ending fit poorly |
| Hebrew נרד and a possible נתן-form association | Nard and giving were explored as component candidates | They do not yield a supported whole reading |
| Arabic nūr association | The beginning can evoke light | The long remainder is unexplained; this is a weak association |

The most useful Latin sound comparison was «ви-те-не-ам» against «ви-те-ни-ум», maintaining в–т–н–м. `vī teneam` was explored as roughly «пусть я удерживаю силой». It is not the recorded form; the vowel differences, missing object and full syntax prevent treating the entire word as a grammatical Latin sentence. The source case records the consulted lexical references.

Keep both negative-scope candidates:

- не-[порядок жизни — lost] → «Порядок жизни не утрачен».
- [непорядок жизни]-lost → «Неустроенность жизни исчезла».

The additional free reconstruction was that a force maintaining disorder has been lost. That causal picture contains an interpretive addition; it is not a complete dictionary decoding of «неордовитениум».
